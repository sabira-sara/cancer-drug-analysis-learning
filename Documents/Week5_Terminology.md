# Introduction to DepMap and PRISM Repurposing

## 1. DepMap (Cancer Dependency Map)

DepMap is a cancer research resource that provides biological and genetic information about cancer cell lines.

**Key information available in DepMap:**

- **Gene Expression:** Shows how much a particular gene is expressed in a cancer cell line.
- **CRISPR Dependency:** Indicates how important a particular gene is for the survival of a cancer cell.
- **Model Information:** Provides details about cancer cell lines, including their unique identifiers and cancer types.

## 2. PRISM Repurposing

PRISM Repurposing is a resource that provides drug-response data across different cancer cell lines.

It helps researchers understand:

- Which compound (drug) was tested?
- Which cancer cell line received the compound?
- What was the measured response of the cancer cell line to the compound?

## 3. Important Terms and Definitions

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Model ID** | A unique identifier for a cancer cell line. |
| **Compound ID** | An identifier assigned to a tested drug or compound. |
| **Response Metric** | A numerical measurement representing the response of a cancer cell line to a drug. |
| **Gene Expression** | The amount of RNA produced from a particular gene. |
| **CRISPR Dependency** | A measurement indicating how strongly a cancer cell depends on a particular gene for survival. |

## 4. Key Takeaway

DepMap helps researchers understand the biological characteristics and genetic dependencies of cancer cell lines, whereas PRISM Repurposing helps researchers investigate how those cell lines respond to different compounds.

Combining these resources can help researchers explore relationships between gene expression, gene dependency, and drug sensitivity.
